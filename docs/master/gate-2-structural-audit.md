# Gate 2 - Structural Funding Deep Audit

## Result

The 19 structurally funded Gate-1 Likely routes split into:

| Structural class | Count | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| A1 - Near-zero expected personal cost | 13 | Tuition is waived/covered or the package is explicitly designed to cover fees plus living; residual risk is low to moderate. |
| A2 - Structural funding but residual risk | 4 | Funding is guaranteed, but international tuition and/or local living costs can still leave a material personal contribution. |
| A3 - Structural package, amount unclear | 2 | Admission includes or normally brings funding, but the offer-level amount is not standardized enough to estimate net cost yet. |

## A1 - Near-zero expected personal cost

### Hong Kong research MPhil routes

The financial arithmetic is unusually clean.

**CUHK**

- 2026/27 PGS: HKD 229,200/year.
- 2027/28 research-postgraduate tuition: HKD 49,500/year.
- Post-tuition amount before living expenses: about **HKD 179,700/year**.

This architecture applies to the staged CUHK BME and Translational Genomics MPhil routes if the studentship is awarded with admission.

**HKUST**

- 2027/28 PGS: HKD 229,620/year.
- 2027/28 research-postgraduate tuition: HKD 49,500/year.
- Post-tuition amount: about **HKD 180,120/year**.

**HKU**

- PGS basic rate from Sep 2026: HKD 19,135/month = HKD 229,620/year.
- 2027/28 full-time research composition fee: HKD 49,500/year.
- Post-tuition amount: about **HKD 180,120/year**.

Hong Kong housing and living costs still matter, but the stipend-minus-tuition structure is strong enough to treat these as A1 rather than merely “funded.”

### KAUST

All admitted Master's students normally receive the KAUST Fellowship:

- full tuition and bench fees;
- included base housing;
- health insurance;
- relocation support;
- MS stipend worth about USD 20,000/year.

This is the cleanest A1 architecture in the expanded universe.

### Weizmann

- all students are exempt from tuition;
- full-time MSc students receive a regular fellowship of NIS 7,000/month for 24 months.

This is also a clean A1 model.

### KAIST

KAIST Scholarship covers:

- full tuition;
- national health insurance;
- monthly stipend.

Around 94% of international students benefit from KAIST financial support. Master's applicants selecting KAIST Scholarship are automatically considered for KGPS, which gives full tuition + KRW 1,000,000/month for four semesters to selected students.

The exact standard stipend depends on college/research arrangement, so residual confidence is slightly lower than KAUST/Weizmann.

### POSTECH

For 2026, the regular Master's TA/RA package is:

- KRW 884,000/month tuition component;
- KRW 966,000/month living allowance;
- KRW 1,850,000/month total.

The university's international-student estimate is about USD 715/month for housing, food, insurance, transport and personal expenses. Actual assistantship varies by lab, but the standard package is consistent with a near-zero personal-cost model.

### UNIST BME

The department currently advertises full financial support for all BME graduate students, including tuition waiver, KRW 7.68m/year support and stipend. Exact stipend mechanics should still be confirmed in an offer.

### University of Toronto BME MASc

For 2026/27 international MASc students in the funded cohort:

- base-tier guaranteed minimum: CAD 44,500-46,499;
- the programme explicitly says this package comprises both programme fees and a living allowance.

This is materially stronger than a generic Canadian “minimum stipend” that still has to absorb a large international tuition bill.

## A2 - Structural funding with residual risk

### UBC Bioinformatics

The programme currently requires an MSc stipend of at least CAD 32,000/year, with supervisor financial commitment.

That is meaningful structural funding, but Vancouver living costs and international fees still need to be subtracted before calling the programme near-zero personal cost.

### McGill BBME

All thesis Master's students are guaranteed at least CAD 24,000/year for two years, including international students. McGill explicitly notes that higher stipends are encouraged for international students because of higher tuition.

This is clearly a funded research degree but not automatically self-sufficient.

### Waterloo BME MASc

Engineering guarantees CAD 18,000/year for the two-year MASc.

For Winter 2026, the standard international Graduate Research Master's tuition is about CAD 7,696/term plus roughly CAD 943/term in incidental fees. At three terms/year, the guaranteed minimum alone is below annual tuition+fees.

Selected international research Master's students may receive IMAE of CAD 2,500/term for up to five terms.

Therefore the programme is structurally funded but **not** a zero-cost route at the minimum package.

### Dalhousie BME MASc

Dalhousie guarantees about CAD 16,000-17,000/year **after tuition and fees** in years 1-2.

That is financially more transparent than many programmes, but the school explicitly says additional funds may be needed to cover Halifax living costs.

## A3 - Structural package but amount unclear

### Guelph MSc Bioinformatics

Thesis MSc students receive a funding package with their offer. It varies by the supervisor's home department and may combine GTA/GRA funding.

Until a specific supervisor/home department is known, there is no responsible single net-cost estimate.

### McMaster BME MASc

McMaster states that full-time MASc students receive department/supervisor funding for 24 months, but a current standardized international package amount has not yet been verified.
