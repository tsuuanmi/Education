# Active PI Deep Audit - Singapore, Hong Kong, Toronto, KAUST

## Scope

This pass replaces the Korea/Japan-first PI ordering after the applicant's environment preference update.

The active regions are now evaluated using the canonical fit specification:

> **Engineering as the foundation. Biological systems as the application domain. Computation and modelling as the transferable core. Research as the mode of learning.**

A PI receives higher priority when the lab provides:

- deep transferable methods rather than only domain proximity;
- a credible way to leverage existing Mechatronics / signals / imaging / genomics / software experience;
- substantial Master's-level research ownership;
- enough breadth to avoid premature identity narrowing;
- visible current research activity and a credible funding/application route.

Cheow is unusually aligned with the applicant's full trajectory.

His formal background is in Electrical Engineering / EECS, while his current research spans:

- single-cell analysis;
- spatio-temporal single-cell platforms;
- next-generation sequencing;
- microfluidics;
- systems biology technologies.

This is a rare engineering-to-genomics trajectory that mirrors the applicant's own transition.

Importantly, the work is visibly active rather than historical. In 2025, NUS reported a major >S$1M research grant led by Cheow on **comprehensive single-cell transcriptomic profiling of cancer chemoresistance**.
