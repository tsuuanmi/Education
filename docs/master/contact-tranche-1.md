# First PI Contact Tranche

## Decision

The first outreach tranche should contain **one PI per institution**, not every strong PI at once.

This reduces two risks:

1. presenting an unfocused research identity to the same department;
2. consuming fallback options before learning how the first PI responds.

## Why Yueming Jin and Jiguang Wang are not in Tranche 1

They are not downgraded.

They are deliberate **institution-level fallback / second-direction options**:

- Yueming Jin gives NUS a multimodal medical-AI branch if Cheow's work is too experimentally oriented.
- Jiguang Wang gives HKUST a quantitative-genomics branch if Hao Chen is unavailable or the desired project should emphasize omics over imaging.

Keeping them in reserve creates optionality without making the first contact wave look scattered.

## Timing

### Toronto

### HKUST / NTU

## First-contact policy

For Tranche 1:

1. attach only the concise research CV;
2. do not attach a long proposal;
3. reference one current project/paper;
4. ask one concrete but flexible research question;
5. ask whether a suitable Master's research path exists;
6. one follow-up after 7-10 days if needed;
7. activate the same-institution fallback only after a negative/no-fit signal, or after sufficient spacing.

## Success criterion

The first email is successful if it resolves one of these:

- PI is interested and asks for a meeting/materials;
- PI confirms a relevant project exists;
- PI says they are not taking Master's students;
- PI redirects to a better colleague;
- PI identifies a mismatch early enough to save application effort.

The objective is **information gain**, not an admission promise.
