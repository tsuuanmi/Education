# Master Program Research

## Objective

Find all realistic Master's and research-Master's paths that can develop the applicant into a Research Engineer able to investigate new biological/technical problems and translate them into validated systems or products.

The desired methodological core is:

- measurement and sensing;
- signals and signal processing;
- estimation / Bayesian inference;
- system identification / dynamical systems;
- computation;
- experimentation and validation;
- research-to-product translation.

BCI / neurotechnology is currently the preferred research application, but the degree should preserve enough methodological breadth that genomics experience remains useful.

## Search space

Degree families:

- Biomedical Engineering / Bioengineering
- Health Technology / Medical Engineering
- Neural Engineering / Neuroengineering / BCI
- Electrical Engineering with biomedical, neural, signals, or sensing focus
- Signals / Systems / Control with biomedical thesis options
- Robotics / human-machine interaction / rehabilitation robotics
- Computational Bioengineering / Life Sciences Engineering
- Research degrees: MS, MEng by Research, MPhil
- Erasmus Mundus / joint degrees

Geographies:

- Nordics
- Continental Europe
- Korea / Japan / Singapore / Hong Kong / Taiwan
- North America / Australia only when a realistic full or near-full funding path exists
