# Gate 2 - A-Band Structural Funding Audit

## Purpose

The `A - Structurally funded` label means funding is tied closely to research admission or broadly provided to admitted students.

It does **not** mean every A-band programme has the same financial outcome.

This audit separates funding architecture from actual residual-cost exposure.

## A-band financial subtiers

### A1 - Financially self-contained

These packages cover tuition and remove or substantially cover major living-cost components.

#### KAUST - Bioengineering / Bioinformatics and Machine Learning

Current KAUST Fellowship:

- full tuition and bench fees;
- approximately USD 20,000/year MS stipend;
- base housing;
- health insurance;
- relocation support.

KAUST reports average student day-to-day spending of about USD 1,320/month. The MS stipend is approximately USD 1,667/month while housing and insurance are separately covered.

**Interpretation:** very low expected personal residual under a normal single-student lifestyle.

Source:
- https://admissions.kaust.edu.sa/fees-funding
- https://admissions.kaust.edu.sa/about-kaust/cost-of-living

#### Weizmann - Computational and Systems Biology MSc

Current structure:

- no tuition;
- all full-time MSc students receive a fellowship for 24 months;
- regular MSc fellowship: NIS 7,000/month.

External fellowships generally substitute for rather than stack with the regular fellowship.

**Interpretation:** clean structural funding with low expected residual, subject to normal housing/lifestyle variation.

Source:
- https://www.weizmann.ac.il/wsos/fellowship-aid/msc-students

### A2 - Structural and likely manageable

Funding is closely coupled to admission and appears broadly sufficient, but local cost or package variation matters.

#### CUHK - BME MPhil

Current evidence:

- 2027/28 research-postgraduate tuition: HKD 49,500/year;
- 2026/27 PGS: HKD 229,200/year;
- PGS is payable during normative study and announced with admission.

Nominal amount after 2027/28 tuition:

```text
229,200 - 49,500 = HKD 179,700/year
≈ HKD 14,975/month before housing/living
```

**Interpretation:** structurally strong and likely financially viable, but final 2027/28 PGS rate and housing should be checked.

Sources:
- https://www.gs.cuhk.edu.hk/admissions/scholarships-fees/studentships
- https://www.gs.cuhk.edu.hk/admissions/scholarships-fees/fees

#### HKUST - Bioengineering MPhil / ECE MPhil

2027/28:

- research-postgraduate tuition: HKD 49,500/year;
- PGS: HKD 229,620/year;
- up to 2 years for full-time MPhil;
- no separate PGS application.

Nominal amount after tuition:

```text
229,620 - 49,500 = HKD 180,120/year
≈ HKD 15,010/month before housing/living
```

**Interpretation:** strong structural funding. The main residual variable is accommodation/lifestyle, not tuition financing.

Sources:
- https://ebookshelf.hkust.edu.hk/flippingbook/G23842_PG_Prospectus/16-17/
- https://registry.hkust.edu.hk/sites/default/files/2025-08/RPG_Tuition_adjustment%20details_Aug25.pdf

#### HKU - ECE MPhil / Computing & Data Science MPhil

Current structure:

- 2027/28 MPhil composition fee: HKD 49,500/year;
- PGS: HKD 19,135/month from September 2026;
- renewable annually during normative study, subject to satisfactory progress and funding availability.

Annualized PGS:

```text
19,135 x 12 = HKD 229,620
229,620 - 49,500 = HKD 180,120/year after tuition
```

Graduate House currently charges HKD 5,584/month for a single room.

**Interpretation:** likely manageable with university housing; private Hong Kong housing can materially worsen the residual.

Sources:
- https://gradsch.hku.hk/prospective_students/fees_scholarships_and_financial_support/postgraduate_scholarships
- https://gradhse.hku.hk/admission-application/

#### KAIST - Bio and Brain Engineering MS

KAIST Scholarship:

- full tuition;
- national health insurance;
- monthly stipend depending on college/research involvement;
- KAIST states that the scholarship guarantees minimum living expenses;
- 94% of international students benefit.

Selected KGPS Master's students receive:

- full tuition;
- KRW 1,000,000/month;
- four regular semesters.

KAIST's published 2023 non-tuition single-student budget was about KRW 11.07m/year.

**Interpretation:** structurally strong. Do not model the KRW 1m KGPS stipend as guaranteed; distinguish standard KAIST Scholarship from KGPS when evaluating the offer.

Sources:
- https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-graduate/FinancialSupport/Scholarship/KAISTScholarship/
- https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-graduate/FinancialSupport/CostofAttendance

#### POSTECH - EE MS / Interdisciplinary Bioscience & Bioengineering MS

2026 regular Master's TA/RA package:

- tuition component: KRW 884,000/month;
- living allowance: KRW 966,000/month;
- total: KRW 1,850,000/month;
- actual amount may vary by lab;
- advisors may add allowance.

POSTECH estimates average living expenses around USD 715/month, and graduate dormitory rooms currently cost roughly KRW 149k-267k/month.

**Interpretation:** viable but relatively close to the university's own living-cost benchmark. Lab-specific top-up should be checked with the PI.

Sources:
- https://adm-g.postech.ac.kr/ENG/graduate-info/support-policy/
- https://postech.ac.kr/eng/student-life/dormitory.do
- https://international.postech.ac.kr/

#### UNIST - Biomedical Engineering MS

The department currently advertises:

- tuition-fee waiver;
- KRW 7,680,000/year and stipend/support;
- full financial support for all BME graduate students.

**Interpretation:** structurally attractive, but the public page does not itemize the current MS cash stipend sufficiently for precise net-cost modelling.

Source:
- https://bme.unist.ac.kr/eng/graduate/

### A3 - Structural funding but material residual risk

Funding is guaranteed or closely linked to admission, but the minimum package may still leave a meaningful personal-cost exposure.

#### University of Toronto - BME MASc

For 2026/27 international MASc students:

- base guaranteed package: CAD 44,500-46,499;
- higher external-award tiers rise to CAD 49,500;
- the package explicitly includes both programme fees and a living allowance;
- guarantee applies during MASc years 1-2.

**Interpretation:** financially much stronger than a normal Canadian coursework Master's, but Toronto living costs are high. Final evaluation should use the exact **net-after-fees** amount in the admission year rather than the gross stipend.

Source:
- https://bme.utoronto.ca/current-students/tuition-and-funding/

#### UBC - Bioinformatics MSc

Current programme data:

- minimum stipend: CAD 31,000/year for all students accepted by a faculty member;
- international tuition: CAD 10,081.65/year before any International Tuition Award;
- current median funding: CAD 33,453/year;
- current median net after tuition/fees: CAD 23,693/year.

At the minimum package, tuition alone leaves approximately:

```text
31,000 - 10,081.65 ≈ CAD 20,918/year
```

before other student fees.

Canada's current single-student living-funds requirement is CAD 23,448/year, while Vancouver's realistic costs can be higher.

**Interpretation:** keep A because funding is structural, but do **not** treat a bare-minimum UBC offer as financially safe. Prefer a PI with above-minimum RA/top-up or additional awards.

Sources:
- https://www.grad.ubc.ca/prospective-students/graduate-degree-programs/master-of-science-bioinformatics
- https://students.ubc.ca/international-student-guide/study-permits/extend-study-permit-within-canada/gather-required-documents-study-permit-extension/

## A-band conclusion

The 13 A-band routes divide into:

| Financial interpretation | Routes |
| --- | --- |
| **A1 - self-contained** | KAUST; Weizmann |
| **A2 - structural, likely manageable** | CUHK BME; HKUST Bioengineering; HKUST ECE; HKU ECE; HKU Computing/Data; KAIST BBE; POSTECH EE; POSTECH I-Bio; UNIST BME |
| **A3 - structural but material residual risk** | Toronto BME MASc; UBC Bioinformatics MSc |

This is **not a research ranking**.

The main lesson is:

> structural funding should be preserved as its own dimension, while residual cost is evaluated separately.

UBC therefore remains structurally attractive even though its minimum package is financially weaker than KAUST or Weizmann.
