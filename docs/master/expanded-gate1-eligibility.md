# Expanded Gate 1 - Formal Eligibility

## Scope

This Gate 1 covers the **87 new/replacement application routes** produced by Discovery Phase 2.0.

It does not yet mutate the old 78-record canonical Gate-1 snapshot.

Gate 1 asks only:

- is the prior degree formally relevant/equivalent?
- are there hard subject-credit prerequisites?
- is direct Master's entry possible?
- is the language/application route actionable?
- does a published minimum GPA/degree-class threshold create a real blocker?

It deliberately does **not** penalise:

- scholarship uncertainty;
- tuition/living cost;
- research competition;
- need to contact a supervisor;
- GRE/IELTS as ordinary application tests;
- the programme being less attractive strategically.

Those belong to later gates.

## Important formal-risk patterns

### Hard biology / life-science credit blockers

Current high-confidence hard blockers include:

- DTU Bioinformatics - mandatory molecular-biology credit;
- University of Copenhagen Bioinformatics - published biology/bioinformatics/ML coursework requirements;
- Saarland Bioinformatics - formal bioinformatics + life-science credits;
- University of Milan Quantitative Biology - formal biological-discipline credits;
- Freie Universität Berlin Bioinformatics - 25 CP biology/chemistry/biochemistry with no conditional admission;
- Clemson Biomedical Data Science and Informatics - one year of college biology.

Professional GeneStory experience is valuable for admission narrative and later differentiation, but Gate 1 does not assume professional experience replaces explicit transcript-credit requirements.

### Formal CS-credit risk

High-risk cases include:

- Uppsala Bioinformatics (CS background);
- McGill Computer Science thesis;
- SFU Computing Science thesis;
- University of Alberta Computing Science thesis;
- TU Wien / MedUni Medical Informatics.

These programmes may be excellent career fits, but the applicant should not be treated as a conventional CS graduate when the university publishes formal CS-course expectations.

### Bridgeable interdisciplinary cases

Some programmes are intentionally designed to accept quantitative/engineering entrants and add missing biology:

- ETH CBB permits additional requirements up to 30 ECTS;
- VU/UvA can route applicants with gaps to a pre-master;
- TU Graz can impose supplementary courses;
- Bern BME explicitly accepts varied backgrounds;
- Dalhousie BME can assign biomedical foundations.

These are different from hard-credit programmes that provide no conditional route.

### Route/language risk in Japan

Japan needs programme-by-programme treatment.

Clean or relatively clean current international routes include:

- UTokyo Computational Biology and Medical Sciences;
- Tsukuba Life Science Innovation;
- NAIST Computational Biology;
- Kyushu Systems Life Sciences international course.

Higher-risk route/language cases include:

- University of Osaka Information Science/Bioinformatic Engineering;
- Hokkaido Bioengineering & Bioinformatics;
- Kyoto Systems Science;
- Kyutech Biomedical Informatics.

For Osaka specifically, the Graduate School is reorganising in April 2027 and most Master's classes outside the Information Technology Special Course in English are basically conducted in Japanese.

## Interpretation

A `Likely` result does **not** mean admission is likely.

It means:

> Based on the currently verified formal rules, there is no known academic-admissibility blocker.

A `Conditional-high-risk` result means:

> A real formal condition could block direct admission, and application effort should wait until that condition is resolved.

An `Unlikely` result means:

> A published hard prerequisite appears absent from the current transcript.

## Conditional-resolution pass 1

Official-source re-checks resolved several first-pass ambiguities:

- **ETH CBB -> Likely:** engineering applicants are eligible; missing introductory material can be assigned as up to 30 ECTS additional requirements.
- **UPF Bioinformatics for Health Sciences -> Likely:** engineering degrees are explicitly accepted.
- **Imperial Biomedical Research (Data Science) -> Likely:** the stream explicitly targets numerate engineering/physical-science backgrounds.
- **JHU BME MSE -> Likely:** engineering/basic-science degrees are standard entry backgrounds and curricular deficiencies can be remediated with additional courses.
- **HKU Computing & Data Science MPhil -> Likely:** the published formal requirement is an honours-equivalent bachelor's degree; no CS-major prerequisite is stated.
- **Trento Quantitative and Computational Biology -> Unlikely:** the current formal curriculum requires 12 ECTS biology and 6 ECTS chemistry that are not documented in the transcript.

Gate 1 therefore now stands at **58 Likely / 22 Conditional-high-risk / 7 Unlikely** for the 87 Discovery-2.0 routes.
