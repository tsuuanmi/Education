# Research Log - 2026-10-04

## Repository initialization

This repository is now the canonical tracker for the Master Program research.

The research process intentionally avoids premature ranking. The goal is to build a broad opportunity universe first, because later constraints can eliminate apparently ideal programmes.

## Important corrections from earlier exploratory reports

Earlier deep-research reports were useful for discovery but contained factual errors. Their rankings are **not** treated as canonical.

Confirmed corrections include:

- Finland does not provide a generic government scholarship for Bachelor's/Master's; university scholarships are the relevant route.
- The Netherlands orientation year is one year, not three.
- Sweden's post-study job-search/start-business permit is up to one year.
- ETH and EPFL funding must be kept separate; ETH ESOP is not an EPFL scholarship.
- Scholarship and immigration claims must be checked against current official sources before entering the decision matrix.
- Faculty names are not stored unless affiliation is verified on a current official university/lab page.

## New Europe discoveries

### TU Delft

Two separate paths deserve tracking:

1. **MSc Biomedical Engineering - Medical Devices**
   - medical devices, microsensors, imaging, bioelectronics, estimation/detection;
   - strong clinically driven research.
2. **MSc Electrical Engineering - Signals, Networking and Sensing**
   - starts in its current form from September 2026;
   - strong mathematical modelling, signal processing, systems theory and sensing;
   - official TU Delft research pages show biomedical applications and thesis work in EEG, ECG, ultrasound, MRI and wearable sensing.

This is an important example of a **methods-first degree with biomedical research**, which matches the desired career identity without locking the degree title to BCI.

Sources:
- https://bioelectronics.tudelft.nl/Education/detail.php?ti=27
- https://sps.ewi.tudelft.nl/Education/detail.php?ti=53
- https://sps.ewi.tudelft.nl/Education/mscstudents.php

### TU Eindhoven

The Biomedical Diagnostics research ecosystem is highly relevant because it explicitly models the **full measurement chain**:

pathophysiological source -> sensing physics -> signal acquisition -> interpretation.

The programme-level admission and finance details still need a dedicated audit.

Sources:
- https://research.tue.nl/en/organisations/biomedical-diagnostics-lab/
- https://research.tue.nl/en/organisations/biomedical-engineering/

### University of Freiburg

**MSc Microsystems Engineering** is a hidden gem:

- explicit admission from Mechatronics and related engineering;
- Biomedical Engineering concentration;
- 120 ECTS / 2 years;
- non-EU fee currently EUR 1,500 tuition + semester charges;
- official living-cost estimate EUR 900-1,000/month;
- fourth-semester thesis in a research group.

Source:
- https://www.tf.uni-freiburg.de/en/study-programs/microsystem-engineering/m-sc-microsystems-engineering-en

### Grenoble SIGMA

Methodological fit is excellent for signal processing / systems / inverse problems, but the current official page says M1 does not open in 2026 and M2 does not open in 2027. It is therefore tracked as a **non-actionable 2027 opportunity**, not as a current application target.

Source:
- https://www.grenoble-inp.fr/en/academics/master-tsi-parcours-signal-image-processing-methods-and-applications-sigma

### France Excellence Eiffel

Current Campus France rules state a Master's living allowance of EUR 1,200/month from January 2026 plus several direct services. Institutions nominate candidates; students do not submit directly to Campus France. For French public national diplomas, government-scholarship recipients may be exempt from tuition under the relevant rules.

Source:
- https://www.campusfrance.org/en/the-france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-program

### KU Leuven

The Faculty of Engineering Science offers a limited automatic fee-waiver assessment for non-EEA students. For 2026-27, the waiver reduced tuition to EUR 3,252.72/year for selected programmes including Biomedical Engineering; 2027-28 values are due later.

Source:
- https://www.kuleuven.be/scholarships/year/2026-2027/faculty-of-engineering-science-fee-waiver
