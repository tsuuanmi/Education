# Research Log - 2026-10-04

## Repository initialization

This repository is now the canonical tracker for the Master Program research.

The research process intentionally avoids premature ranking. The goal is to build a broad opportunity universe first, because later constraints can eliminate apparently ideal programmes.

## Important corrections from earlier exploratory reports

Earlier deep-research reports were useful for discovery but contained factual errors. Their rankings are **not** treated as canonical.

Confirmed corrections include:

- Finland does not provide a generic government scholarship for Bachelor's/Master's; university scholarships are the relevant route.
- The Netherlands orientation year is one year, not three.
- Sweden's post-study job-search/start-business permit is up to one year.
- ETH and EPFL funding must be kept separate; ETH ESOP is not an EPFL scholarship.
- Scholarship and immigration claims must be checked against current official sources before entering the decision matrix.
- Faculty names are not stored unless affiliation is verified on a current official university/lab page.

## New Europe discoveries

### TU Delft

Two separate paths deserve tracking:

1. **MSc Biomedical Engineering - Medical Devices**
   - medical devices, microsensors, imaging, bioelectronics, estimation/detection;
   - strong clinically driven research.
2. **MSc Electrical Engineering - Signals, Networking and Sensing**
   - starts in its current form from September 2026;
   - strong mathematical modelling, signal processing, systems theory and sensing;
   - official TU Delft research pages show biomedical applications and thesis work in EEG, ECG, ultrasound, MRI and wearable sensing.

This is an important example of a **methods-first degree with biomedical research**, which matches the desired career identity without locking the degree title to BCI.

Sources:
- https://bioelectronics.tudelft.nl/Education/detail.php?ti=27
- https://sps.ewi.tudelft.nl/Education/detail.php?ti=53
- https://sps.ewi.tudelft.nl/Education/mscstudents.php

### RWTH Aachen Biomedical Engineering

A new strong low-tuition path:

- English interdisciplinary MSc across medicine, engineering and natural sciences;
- 4 semesters;
- quantitative physiology and therapeutic medical systems are part of the teaching ecosystem;
- the programme includes practical medical-device development / verification projects;

Admission fit remains **uncertain** until the programme-specific academic-content requirements are mapped against the UET transcript.

### FAU Medical Engineering

FAU adds a useful engineering-first alternative:

- English specialisations include **Medical Robotics**, Medical Image and Data Processing, and Health & Medical Data Analytics;
- 120 ECTS / 4 semesters with research/lab work and a 30-ECTS thesis;
- related technical bachelor degrees with strong mathematics, computer science and electrical engineering are targeted;
- from summer semester 2027, non-EU tuition is **EUR 4,000/semester**;
- summer 2027 application window is 15 Sep 2026 - 15 Jan 2027.

Financially weaker than the no-tuition German public options, but still worth retaining before the finance gate because Medical Robotics fits the target Research Engineer identity.

Sources:
- https://www.medical-engineering.study.fau.eu/prospective-students/joining-the-master-degree-program/
- https://www.tf.fau.eu/degree-program/medical-engineering-m-sc/

### VUB Biomedical Engineering

This is a notable BCI-specific option without making the whole degree BCI-only:

- English, 120 ECTS;
- profiles include **Neuro-engineering**, Sensors and Medical Devices, AI and Digital Health;
- Neuro-engineering includes biomedical product development, medical imaging, data analytics and a 24-ECTS thesis;

### KU Leuven funding upgrade

KU Leuven BME is more financially interesting than previously recorded:

- Biomedical Engineering was eligible for **Master Mind** in 2026/27;
- Master Mind provided full tuition waiver + EUR 10,225/year for up to 2 years;
- Vietnamese applicants were also eligible for the **KU Leuven ASEAN Scholarship**, which provided full tuition waiver + EUR 4,000 one-time allowance;
- the Faculty of Engineering Science separately offered limited tuition waivers.

Critical constraint: preparatory/bridging programmes are not eligible for Master Mind or KU Leuven ASEAN scholarship. Therefore direct admission versus preparatory admission changes the financial viability materially.

Sources:
- https://www.kuleuven.be/scholarships/year/2026-2027/eligible-programmes-2026-2027
- https://www.kuleuven.be/scholarships/year/2026-2027/master-mind-scholarship

## Italy + scholarship-first Europe

### Politecnico di Milano

Biomedical Engineering remains a strong finance-sensitive option because it has three independent funding channels:

- international merit-based scholarships;
- DSU right-to-study aid;
- Invest Your Talent in Italy (IYT) for eligible Vietnamese applicants/programmes.

Polimi confirms that DSU can include a cash component, catering, and discounted accommodation; applications are made under an annual call and can be submitted even before final enrolment.

Sources:
- https://www.polimi.it/en/prospective-students/how-much-does-it-cost/scholarships
- https://www.polimi.it/en/students/tuition-fees-scholarships-and-financial-aid/university-financial-aid-diritto-allo-studio-universitario-dsu

### Invest Your Talent in Italy

IYT is now tracked as a country-level funding route rather than a programme-specific scholarship.

For 2026/27:

- Vietnam was an eligible country;
- the programme covered Master's and second-level Master's study in Engineering & Advanced Technologies, Architecture & Design, and Economics & Management;
- a mandatory internship at an Italian company/trade association was part of the programme;
- Vietnam had 245 submitted applications, 160 admitted applications, and 35 awarded scholarships.

The 2026/27 Vietnam deadline was 11 May 2026. For a 2027/28 start, the next call must be monitored rather than assumed unchanged.

Sources:
- https://ambhanoi.esteri.it/en/news/dall_ambasciata/2026/04/call-for-applications-for-the-invest-your-talent-in-italy-scholarship-program-for-the-2026-2027-academic-year/
- https://investyourtalentapplication.esteri.it/SitoIYT/EN/countries?Paese=VIETNAM

### University of Bologna Biomedical Engineering

The 2026/27 programme is fully in English, 120 ECTS, and explicitly offers a **Biomedical Engineering for Neuroscience** curriculum.

Confirmed relevant courses include:

- Neural Systems;
- Neuroscience and Cognition;
- Computational Neuroimaging.

This is one of the clearest examples of **BCI/neuroscience depth inside a broader BME degree**.

Funding is structurally different from merit-only scholarships: ER.GO provides a regional right-to-study route based on economic conditions and merit. International family income/assets documentation is a key administrative requirement.

Sources:
- https://www.unibo.it/en/study/second-cycle-degree/programme/2026/6705
- https://www.unibo.it/en/study/course-units-transferable-skills-moocs/course-unit-catalogue/course-unit/2026/535988

### University of Pisa / Scuola Superiore Sant'Anna - Bionics Engineering

This is a major hidden gem for the target profile:

- Bionics Engineering sits at the intersection of biorobotics, neural engineering, signal processing and human-machine systems;
- the Sant'Anna **Second-Level Ordinary Course** is a separate competitive route layered on top of the Master's;
- for 2026/27, reserved places existed for Bionics Engineering;
- winners study tuition-free at Sant'Anna and receive free campus housing and meals;
- the competition required a degree completed in no more than four years and an average >=27/30 or foreign equivalent.

The main uncertainty is whether the applicant's 3.47/4.0 transcript converts to the required equivalent threshold and whether the Bionics Engineering curriculum accepts the exact UET course distribution.

Source:
- https://www.santannapisa.it/en/news/concorso-ammissione-scuola-santanna-2026-2027

### University of Genoa - Bioengineering

A particularly strong new programme:

- English, 120 ECTS;
- current curriculum includes Analysis of Biomedical Data and Signals, Bioinformatics, Biomedical Robotics, Biosensors and Microsystems, Rehabilitation Engineering, AI in Medicine, Biomedical Imaging and Cellular/Tissue Engineering;
- programme applications explicitly span neuro-technologies, assistive/rehabilitation systems, instrumentation, e-health and m-health;

This is one of the strongest **BCI + genomics optionality** programmes found so far.
