# Contact Readiness - Tranche 1

## Why this gate exists

A strong thematic PI match is not enough.

Before outreach is considered send-ready, distinguish:

- **research-active**;
- **publicly recruiting researchers**;
- **confirmed to accept Master's students**;
- **fundable through the target programme**.

Do not turn a generic hiring signal into a claim that a PI has an open Master's slot.
