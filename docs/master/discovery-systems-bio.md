# Discovery Phase 2.0 - Systems-Oriented Computational / Dry-Lab Biological Research Engineering

## Why this phase exists

The first opportunity search was biased toward biomedical engineering, neural engineering, sensing and BCI because those domains matched the applicant's undergraduate thesis and Earable experience.

That search remains useful, but it is not a complete search of the applicant's actual Research Engineer identity.

The applicant also has several years of genomics R&D at GeneStory and active scientific-software work on DNA. The new search therefore starts from the **operating model**, not from BCI or genomics as a fixed domain:

> **systems-oriented, computational / dry-lab Research Engineering for biological systems**

No programme in this document is shortlisted yet.

## Discovery question

For each programme, ask:

> Can this programme turn an engineering/software background plus real biological-domain experience into strong quantitative research capability that remains portable across biological systems?

Good programmes may operate on:

- genomes / omics;
- neural and physiological signals;
- health/clinical data;
- medical images;
- biological networks;
- molecular or cellular systems;
- multimodal biological measurements.

The methods layer is more important than the biological object.

## What counts as in-scope

### Family A - Computational biology / bioinformatics

Algorithms, statistics, software, genomics/omics, data infrastructure, computational experiments.

### Family B - Systems / quantitative biology

Dynamic models, networks, simulation, inference, complexity, multiscale biological systems.

### Family C - Biomedical / health data science

Health informatics, biomedical AI, clinical data, precision health, digital medicine.

### Family D - Scientific computation inside engineering or computing

CS/ECE/BME research degrees where the lab applies computation, signals, modelling or software to biological questions.

### Family E - Research-intensive conversion routes

Programmes deliberately designed to let quantitative/engineering students acquire enough biology while retaining a computational identity.

## Breadth test

A candidate is especially interesting when it trains several portable capabilities:

```text
algorithms / statistics / ML
+ scientific programming / software
+ data systems / HPC
+ modelling / simulation
+ research design / validation
+ substantial thesis or supervised research
```

A genomics-heavy degree is still in scope if these capabilities dominate the training.

A programme is less attractive when the degree's value depends mostly on wet-lab molecular techniques or on one narrow disease/omics niche.

## Timing reset

2028 is a fully acceptable start year.

The planning objective is no longer "find something good enough for 2027". It is:

> choose the best feasible 2027 or 2028 opportunity after programme, funding and supervisor evidence are complete.

This makes several routes materially more important:

- Australia Awards 2028;
- scholarship cycles not open yet;
- programmes where test preparation or supervisor outreach benefits from another year;
- funded Canadian / research-Master routes requiring early supervisor commitment.

## Wave 1 findings

The staging CSV currently contains candidates across Europe, UK, Canada, Australia and Asia. They are intentionally **not ranked**.

### Particularly important structural discoveries

**Aalto Life Science Technologies - Bioinformatics and Digital Health**

This is much broader than a traditional genomics degree. The current curriculum combines probabilistic modelling, ML, computational genomics, biological networks, digital health, medical imaging and AI in health technologies.

**ETH Computational Biology & Bioinformatics**

A flexible joint programme across ETH/UZH/Basel with engineering explicitly accepted. It is methods-rich and research-heavy, but the biology/bioinformatics prerequisite and GRE need Gate-1 attention.

**Utrecht Bioinformatics & Biocomplexity**

A strong expression of the new North Star: biological big data plus mathematical/computational models of dynamic living systems, with a 51-ECTS major research project.

**KU Leuven Bioinformatics - Engineering Major**

Notable because the degree explicitly preserves an Engineering major and uses a reorientation package to add missing biology rather than assuming a biology-first applicant.

**University of Milan / Politecnico di Milano - Bioinformatics for Computational Genomics**

The title is genomics-specific, but the curriculum is broader than the title: scientific programming, ML, databases, Spark/cloud, systems biology/network analysis, genomic big-data management and an interdisciplinary research internship. Engineering/math/physics entrants receive biology alignment courses.

**UBC MSc Bioinformatics**

One of the strongest new discoveries for the finance + research combination. UBC explicitly allows Engineering/Physics/CS/Math applicants with significant life-science experience, and work experience may be considered. Current programme funding states a minimum MSc stipend of CAD 32,000/year. GeneStory may be especially valuable in satisfying the life-science-experience side.

**HKU Computing & Data Science MPhil**

Potentially a major option because the degree remains Computing/Data Science while research can be in bioinformatics. The school lists bioinformatics alongside ML/AI, algorithms, systems and software engineering; current PGS support is at least HKD 225,120/year.

**KAIST Bio & Brain Engineering - reframed**

This programme should no longer be treated as a neuro-only target. Official KAIST research areas include Bioinformatics/Systems Biology, Bioelectronics, Neural Engineering and Biomedical Imaging, while the curriculum includes Genome Bioinformatics, Systems Biology, Data Mining and Bio-Intelligence. It may be one of the strongest single programmes for cross-domain optionality.

**University of Tokyo Computational Biology and Medical Sciences**

A distinct route from UTokyo Bioengineering G30. The department's current admissions material supports a Computational Biology Master's, and its FAQ notes that engineering-background students have completed the programme.

**NTU Singapore MSc Biomedical Data Science**

A broad applied route with Bioinformatics, Biotechnology and AI tracks and a possible research immersion. It deserves discovery-stage attention because the separate NTU VinGroup Graduate Scholarship remains active for Vietnamese Master's students; exact programme eligibility must be verified.

**NTU Taiwan Biomedical Electronics and Bioinformatics**

Strategically interesting because it bridges sensing/electronics and bioinformatics in the degree itself rather than forcing a choice between the Earable and GeneStory parts of the profile.

**Australia**

Australia is now a first-class 2028 search region, especially:
- UQ Bioinformatics Research Extensive;
- Monash Bioinformatics Research Stream;
- ANU Quantitative Biology & Bioinformatics Advanced;
- UNSW MPhil BME with Digital Medicine & Digital Biology research.

## Funding architecture update

### Australia Awards

Treat 2028 as a first-class route. The exact 2028 call is not yet published and must be refreshed when available.

### Vingroup

Two different programmes must not be conflated:

1. The general **Vingroup Science & Technology Scholarship for Overseas Study** states that from Cohort 2026 it accepts Doctoral degrees only.
2. The separate **VinGroup Graduate Scholarship at NTU Singapore** is currently active for Vietnamese Master's/PhD candidates and covers tuition/fees, a Master's stipend of S$1,500/month, settling allowance and airfare, with a Vietnam service obligation.

Because return to Vietnam is acceptable, the NTU-specific route may substantially change the economics of eligible NTU Master's programmes.

### Canada research Master's

UBC demonstrates why Canada deserves a dedicated pass: a thesis Master's can have supervisor-backed structural funding rather than relying on a separate scholarship competition.

## Wave 2 - funded computing degrees and quantitative-biology coverage

Wave 2 deliberately searched beyond programmes with "bioinformatics" in the degree title.

### Canada: research funding + broad degree identity

**Simon Fraser University - MSc Computing Science**

This is an important archetype for the new search. The degree remains Computing Science, while the research ecosystem explicitly includes computational biology and medical/health informatics: genome sequencing, gene regulation, epigenomics, single-cell assays, medical images and medical records. Every Master's student is currently guaranteed at least CAD 24,000/year for two years.

The main issue is Gate 1, not strategic fit: non-CS applicants need demonstrable third-year competence in operating systems, data structures/algorithms and databases.

**McGill - MSc Computer Science (Thesis), Bioinformatics**

Another breadth-preserving route. The degree remains Computer Science and the Bioinformatics thesis option focuses on experimental design, data-analysis tools, modelling, biological databases, algorithms, AI and statistics. Current admissions require at least an undergraduate-minor-equivalent CS background and university minimum 3.2/4.0.

Funding exists, but the exact thesis-MSc package still needs a dedicated school/supervisor audit.

**University of Guelph - MSc Bioinformatics**

The thesis MSc explicitly accepts life, physical, statistical, mathematical and computational-science backgrounds. An advisor must agree before application; admission is not issued without one. Thesis students receive a funding package with the offer, although the amount varies by the supervisor's home department.

This is different from a scholarship lottery: funding is part of the supervisor-backed thesis-offer structure.

### Continental Europe: quantitative/systems routes

**University of Trento - Quantitative and Computational Biology**

The current curriculum is unusually aligned with the target operating model: AI for data analysis, scientific programming, biostatistics, genomic data science, multi-omics, network analysis, knowledge graphs, modelling/simulation, reproducible computational workflows, data-quality/model-assumption evaluation, internship and thesis.

It remains a discovery candidate even though the later Gate-1 audit may find biology/chemistry-credit problems.

**Paris-Saclay - GENIOMHE-AI**

The M1/M2 path combines genomics with algorithms, programming, data science, AI, statistics, databases and systems modelling. Career outcomes explicitly include data scientist, ML engineer and research engineer, so the methods layer is not genomics-only.

**University of Milan - Quantitative Biology**

Useful coverage candidate, but the programme is more biophysical/wet-biological than most Phase-2 targets: compulsory cell biophysics, advanced molecular biology and structural biology sit beside mathematical modelling, Python and AI. This is exactly why discovery and gating must remain separate.

**Freie Universitat Berlin - Bioinformatics**

Worth staging because the programme is methods-heavy and German public-university economics can be attractive, but its hard formal CS-credit requirements may be difficult for a Mechatronics transcript. No conditional admission is offered for missing requirements.

## Discovery lesson from Wave 2

The search should explicitly include the pattern:

```text
broad technical degree
+ biological research lab
+ thesis
+ structural funding
```

not only:

```text
degree title contains "bioinformatics" or "biomedical"
```

SFU and McGill are especially useful examples of why this matters for career breadth.


## Wave 3 - technical-degree-first biological research

Wave 3 looked for programmes where the **degree identity itself remains technical** and biology enters through the thesis/research environment.

**University of Alberta - MSc Computing Science**

The thesis MSc is particularly relevant because departmental funding for full-time MSc students currently has a CAD 30,800 minimum, with an additional component for international tuition. The department accepts related-discipline applicants when their CS background is bridgeable. Current Computing Science faculty include active bioinformatics work on algorithms motivated by multi-omics, theoretical computational biology and omics.

This route is strategically valuable even if it later fails Gate 1: it demonstrates the preferred architecture of a broad CS degree plus biological-systems research.

**Monash - Master of Data Science, Research Stream**

This is a different Australia archetype from the Master of Bioinformatics. The degree remains Data Science and can lead into a Master's thesis after strong in-course performance. Monash has computational-biology/bioinformatics researchers inside Data Science & AI.

The trade-off is important: entry to the research stream is not guaranteed at initial admission, so the programme must later be evaluated as a conditional research route rather than assumed to provide a thesis automatically.

### Search principle strengthened again

The most career-portable programme may have a title such as:

- Computing Science;
- Computer Science;
- Data Science;
- Electrical / Computer Engineering;

while the **thesis** is on genomics, physiological data, computational biology or another biological system.

Discovery therefore continues to search both from the degree side and from the lab/research-area side.
