# Applicant Positioning - Cross-Layer Biological Systems Research Engineer

## One-sentence identity

> **An engineer who builds computational systems, pipelines and tools for biological data, working across measurement, software, modelling and validation while collaborating with domain experts.**

The biological modality can change. The engineering role stays coherent.

## Why the profile is unusual

The applicant already spans layers that are often separated across people or teams:

| Layer | Existing evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Physical systems | Mechatronics, electronics, sensors, control |
| Human/physiological data | EEG thesis, Earable neurotechnology |
| Biological molecular data | Several years of genomics R&D at GeneStory |
| Software | Open-source DNA scientific-software platform |
| Quantitative reasoning | mathematics, statistics, AI, modelling |
| Research translation | research award, public outputs, product-oriented R&D |

A pure CS applicant may be stronger in formal algorithms/software theory.

A pure biology/genetics applicant may be stronger in molecular mechanisms and wet-lab knowledge.

A pure EE/Mechatronics applicant may be stronger in hardware/control.

The applicant's advantage is **integration across those layers**.

## Target professional role

The long-term role is not necessarily:

- bioinformatician;
- geneticist;
- neuroscientist;
- software engineer;
- hardware engineer.

A better umbrella is:

- Research Engineer for Biological Systems;
- Computational Bioengineering Research Engineer;
- Biological Data / Scientific Systems Engineer;
- Biomedical Research Software / Data Systems Engineer;
- Research Engineer in Digital Biology / Precision Health / Quantitative Biology.

Exact job titles can vary by organisation.

## Collaboration model

The applicant does not need to replace the domain scientist.

A strong team may look like:

```text
geneticist / neuroscientist / clinician / experimental biologist
                         |
                         v
             domain question + evidence
                         |
                         v
              applicant's core layer
      data representation / QC / pipelines
        modelling / algorithms / software
       reproducibility / system validation
                         |
                         v
                interpretable insight
                         |
                         v
              domain-expert validation
```

This makes domain collaboration a feature, not a weakness.

## Master's selection principle

The best Master's is one where interdisciplinarity is **institutionalised in the curriculum or lab structure**.

Prefer:

1. engineering-oriented bioinformatics / computational biology;
2. computational bioengineering;
3. biomedical data science with rigorous systems/software/statistics;
4. quantitative/systems biology designed for quantitative entrants;
5. bioelectronics + bioinformatics programmes;
6. broad life-science technology programmes with multiple computational/engineering tracks;
7. research Master's in engineering/computing where biological systems are a serious thesis area.

Use caution with:

- pure CS degrees with heavy formal-CS admission assumptions;
- pure biology/genetics degrees with heavy molecular prerequisites;
- narrow genomics degrees whose portable computational skills are weak;
- narrow neuro programmes where the systems/data layer is secondary.

## What the Master's should add

The applicant already has breadth. The degree should add **rigour and a recognised interdisciplinary frame**.

Highest-value additions are:

- algorithms for biological data;
- probabilistic/statistical modelling;
- machine learning with scientific validation;
- scientific computing / HPC;
- workflow and data-system design;
- quantitative/systems biology;
- research methodology;
- reproducibility and benchmarking;
- substantial thesis work;
- enough biological foundations to communicate effectively with domain experts.

The goal is not to become a shallow generalist. It is to become a **deep systems/computational specialist who is biologically literate across domains**.

## Admission narrative

A strong application story is:

```text
I began with physical systems and measurement in Mechatronics.
I then worked with noisy physiological evidence in neurotechnology.
I later moved to genomic evidence and scientific software.
Across these domains, the recurring problem has been the same:
how to turn complex biological measurements into reliable,
reproducible and useful computational systems.

I now want formal training that deepens the computational,
statistical and systems methods behind that work, while keeping
the biological application domain open.
```

This narrative uses both Earable and GeneStory rather than forcing one to be a detour.

## Future evaluation dimensions

When Discovery Phase 2.0 is complete, programmes should eventually be evaluated on:

1. formal eligibility;
2. funding / net cost;
3. research depth;
4. methods portability;
5. biological-data relevance;
6. **profile differentiation**;
7. thesis / PI quality;
8. career breadth;
9. timing (2027 vs 2028);
10. scholarship obligations.

Profile differentiation should be explicit rather than hidden inside a generic "fit" score.


## Amplification principle

The Master's is not primarily a remediation degree.

It should **amplify** the applicant's existing profile:

```text
Mechatronics
+ sensing / hardware awareness
+ physiological signals
+ medical imaging
+ genomic data
+ scientific software
+ systems thinking
```

into a clearer and more rigorous professional identity.

A programme is strategically strong when admission reviewers can interpret the previous path as evidence that the applicant already works across layers and modalities.

A programme is strategically weaker when the applicant must explain away most of the prior profile and compete mainly on a dimension where conventional applicants are predictably stronger.

Examples:

- pure CS Master's: may flatten the profile into a weaker formal-CS applicant;
- pure molecular biology/genetics Master's: may flatten it into a weaker formal-biology applicant;
- computational bioengineering / BME informatics / biological information engineering: can turn the same mixed background into evidence of readiness.

The ideal Master's therefore **adds depth without erasing breadth**.
