# Tier-A PI Outreach Briefs

## Purpose

These are **research-positioning briefs**, not generic networking notes.

Each outreach should demonstrate three things:

1. the applicant understands the PI's current research direction;
2. the applicant has concrete prior evidence that makes the proposed question credible;
3. the Master's would add new methodological depth rather than simply repeat previous work.

The message should remain short. The research question is a conversation starter, not a claim that the applicant has already designed the thesis.

## Reusable first-contact structure

Every Tier-A email should follow the same five-part logic:

```text
1. Why this PI specifically
2. One-sentence applicant trajectory
3. One concrete research question
4. Why existing evidence makes the question credible
5. Ask about Master's supervision / project availability
```

Avoid:

- long autobiography;
- listing every job;
- attaching a 5-page unsolicited proposal;
- generic praise;
- claiming a fixed thesis topic before discussion.
