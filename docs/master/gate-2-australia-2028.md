# Gate 2 - Australia Awards 2028 Audit

## Status

Australia Awards 2028 is a **first-class funding branch**, not a backup.

Exact 2028 rules are not yet published. This audit uses the current **2027 Vietnam Country Profile** as the planning baseline and marks every rule that must be refreshed.

## Current 2027 baseline

Australia Awards Vietnam currently offers awards for:

- Master's by coursework;
- Master's by research.

The current Vietnam Country Profile lists **Private Sector** as an eligible employment group.

For private-sector applicants, the 2027 baseline is:

- GPA: 7.0 under the Australia Awards Vietnam country criterion;
- at least 24 months relevant full-time work experience in Vietnam by the closing date;
- IELTS Academic 6.0 with no band below 5.5, or accepted TOEFL/PTE equivalent, at application.

The applicant's actual GPA conversion to the scholarship's 10-point criterion must be verified rather than assumed.

## Important correction about Master's by Research

Older Australia Awards Vietnam material can still be found online stating that Master's-by-Research applicants must be lecturers/researchers at universities or research institutes.

The current **2027 Country Profile and current FAQ** are more recent and do not state that restriction. They explicitly:

- offer Master's coursework/research;
- list Private Sector among the standard applicant groups;
- explain additional supporting documents for Master's-by-Research applicants.

Planning should therefore use the current 2027 documents rather than the legacy page.

The 2028 Country Profile must be checked again when released.

## Scholarship benefits

The current 2027 package covers:

- full academic fees;
- return airfare;
- visa and medical costs;
- establishment allowance;
- ongoing contribution to living expenses;
- OSHC;
- supplementary academic support;
- completion travel;
- fieldwork support for eligible research Master's / compulsory coursework fieldwork.

This makes the personal-cost architecture strong enough to treat an AAS-funded Australian programme as near-zero residual.

## Field alignment

Current priority areas include:

- Digital Transformation: AI, Big Data, Robotics and Automation;
- Health: health policy/services, public health, mental health.

The list is explicitly non-exhaustive, and fields outside the list (except Business Administration) may be considered on merit.

The applicant's intended programme narrative should therefore avoid sounding like a disconnected career pivot.

A coherent framing is:

> **digital and computational infrastructure for healthcare / biological data in Vietnam**

with prior evidence from:

- Mechatronics and sensing;
- physiological data / Earable;
- medical imaging / VinBigData;
- genomic data / GeneStory;
- scientific software / DNA.

This can plausibly connect both Health and Digital Transformation.

## Current Australia 2028 staging routes

### Research-degree routes

**UNSW MPhil Engineering - Biomedical Engineering / Digital Medicine & Digital Biology**

Strong AAS narrative because the programme can directly support research on healthcare-data systems. A Master's-by-Research AAS application would require:

- research proposal;
- two referee reports;
- publications list;
- proposed supervisor;
- evidence of supervisor contact;
- evidence of previous research and outcomes.

The applicant already has substantially more research evidence than a generic coursework applicant.

**Monash Master of Engineering Science (Research)**

Also naturally compatible with the research route if the selected project is biomedical/health-data oriented.

The key task is finding a supervisor/project that makes the Vietnam-development story concrete rather than generic engineering research.

### Research-extensive / research-stream coursework routes

- UQ Master of Bioinformatics (Research Extensive)
- Monash Master of Bioinformatics - Research Stream
- Monash Master of Data Science - Research Stream

Their exact AAS classification as coursework vs research should be confirmed from the CRICOS/course offer and 2028 application instructions.

Financially, either classification can be eligible under current 2027 rules; the supporting-document burden differs.

### Broad coursework / systems routes

- UNSW Master of Engineering Science - Biomedical Engineering
- QUT Master of Data Science - Biomedical Data Science
- QUT Master of Biomedical Systems and Technology
- University of Sydney Master of Digital Health and Data Science
- Monash Master of Health Data Analytics

All are currently treated as **AAS-dependent 2028 routes**, not self-funded targets.

## Why 2028 improves the plan

Waiting for 2028 can create several benefits:

1. another year of GeneStory / DNA research and public outputs;
2. stronger evidence of system-building across healthcare-data modalities;
3. time to prepare English scores without rushing;
4. time to contact supervisors for research degrees;
5. a clearer Vietnam-development plan;
6. an opportunity to align a research proposal with the user's existing Australia-linked professional network.

The return-to-Vietnam requirement is not currently treated as a negative. The user is comfortable returning and has an existing professional context that can make the two-year return period productive.

When the 2028 Country Profile is published, verify:

Until then, the Australia routes remain:

> **financially strong if AAS 2028 is won; financially unattractive to self-fund by default.**
