## Hard formal-prerequisite findings

### Clear Gate-1 drops

**DTU Bioinformatics**

International applicants need at least 5 ECTS molecular biology, 5 programming, 5 mathematics and 5 statistics inside a larger relevant-credit basket. The canonical transcript has no clear molecular-biology course.

**University of Copenhagen Bioinformatics - 2027/28**

The new requirements demand 10 ECTS mathematics, 10 ECTS biology/molecular biology/genetics/biochemistry, 5 ECTS programming, 10 ECTS bioinformatics and 5 ECTS machine learning. The biology + bioinformatics academic-credit requirements are not currently met.

**Saarland Bioinformatics**

Requires a comparable Bioinformatics Bachelor plus formal preparation including 18 CP bioinformatics and 10 CP life sciences. Current professional genomics experience does not substitute for those published academic prerequisites.

**Freie Universität Berlin Bioinformatics**

Requires all three hard baskets: 25 CP CS, 25 CP mathematics/statistics and 25 CP biology/chemistry/biochemistry, including molecular-biology/genetics content. No conditional admission is offered.

**Clemson Biomedical Data Science and Informatics**

Engineering applicants are welcome, but one full year of college biology is mandatory and is not evidenced in the transcript.

**University of Milan Quantitative Biology**

The current programme is substantially biology/biotechnology-oriented at entry; no clean engineering conversion path has yet been established.

## Important high-risk rather than drop cases

**Uppsala Bioinformatics - CS background**

The 2027 route requires 25 credits math/stat and 30 credits CS. The mathematical side is plausible; the 30-credit formal-CS requirement needs a module-level conversion.

**University of Toronto MScAC - Data Science for Biology**

Engineering is explicitly eligible, but expected preparation includes third-year algorithms, upper-level systems and at least one upper-level biology course. Exceptional applicants may receive conditional admission, so this is high-risk rather than automatically ineligible.

**TU Wien / MedUni Medical Informatics**

The programme is highly aligned conceptually, but formal entry requires 25 ECTS algorithms/data structures/programming plus 15 ECTS CS foundations, in addition to math/stat. Supplementary exams can cover up to 30 ECTS, so a transcript map is essential.

**Tübingen Medical Informatics**

Applicants from comparable informatics fields can study in English. A Mechatronics degree may be treated as non-comparable, which would normally trigger German C1; this must be resolved before application.

**University of Osaka IST**

There is a 2027 international-selection structure and an English Information Technology Special Course, but the former Bioinformatic Engineering organisation is being restructured from April 2027. The exact biological-information route through the English programme is not yet sufficiently clear.

## Positive formal-fit patterns

Discovery also found a large class of programmes where the interdisciplinary profile is formally normal rather than exceptional:

- Imperial Computational Bioengineering accepts Engineering/Physical Sciences/Mathematics;
- Manchester Bioinformatics & Systems Biology accepts physical sciences, computing and mathematics;
- McMaster BME MASc accepts Engineering/Sciences and deliberately requires engineering students to add Health Sciences coursework;

- KAUST Bioengineering accepts relevant Engineering/Math/Physical/Chemical/Biological Science degrees;

This is exactly the **Master-as-amplifier** pattern the new discovery phase was designed to find.
