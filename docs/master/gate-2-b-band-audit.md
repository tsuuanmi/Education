# Gate 2 - B-Band Competitive Funding Audit

## Definition

`B - Strongly fundable` means:

> a credible full or near-full funding route exists, but funding is a separate competitive layer rather than an automatic consequence of admission.

This is deliberately different from A-band structural funding.

For every B-band route, the relevant financial decision is binary:

```text
funded scenario -> potentially excellent
unfunded scenario -> often financially unacceptable
```

A large nominal scholarship is not the same thing as high funding probability.

## Current B-band count

After the full audit, **35 of the 103 Gate-1 Likely routes** remain B-band.

Several routes were deliberately downgraded:

- Tohoku BME: B -> C
- Tsukuba LSI Bioinformatics: B -> C
- Bologna BME: B -> C
- Milan/Polimi Bioinformatics for Computational Genomics: B -> C
- Kansas Computational Bioengineering MS: B -> D

The downgrades prevent generic university-level scholarship language from being mistaken for a programme-specific full-funding route.

## 1. Singapore research scholarships

### NUS Research Scholarship

For international Master's (Research):

- full tuition waiver;
- S$2,900/month from January 2026;
- typical award period up to 2 years;
- Graduate Assistantship duties;
- no specific post-graduation service bond.

NUS's own on-campus living estimate is roughly S$1,200-S$2,100/month.

**Interpretation:** funded scenario is very strong. The reason these programmes remain B rather than A is scholarship selection, not package quality.

Applies to:
- NUS BME MEng Research
- NUS ECE MEng Research

Source:
- https://nusgs.nus.edu.sg/scholarships/nus-research-scholarship

### NTU Research Scholarship

For international research Master's:

- tuition covered;
- S$2,900/month from January 2026;
- up to 2 years;

Applies to:
- NTU EEE MEng Research

Source:
- https://www.ntu.edu.sg/admissions/graduate/financialmatters/scholarships/rss

**Interpretation:** financially viable if eligible, but much tighter than NTU Research Scholarship.

Source:

## 2. Erasmus Mundus

The common funded architecture is excellent:

- participation/tuition costs covered;
- EUR1,400/month for up to 24 months;
- travel/visa/installation support under EMJM rules.

The weakness is **funding robustness**.

### EMJM Imaging

- around 15 scholarships/year;
- 1,108 applications in the first 2026 round.

The raw ratio is not an individual probability, but it demonstrates the magnitude of competition.

### MAIA

Erasmus Mundus funding is renewed for the 2027-29 edition.

Tentative scholarship window:
- November 2026-February 2027.

### EMMaH

Funding structure is verified from the previous scholarship campaign, but the specific 2027-29 scholarship call is not yet published.

### EMMBIOME

The EMJM project remains active through 2028, but exact 2027 scholarship count/call still needs confirmation.

### IPCVAI

2027-29 call:
- 1 November 2026-2 February 2027.

Programme reports a cohort of about 25 from more than 1,600 applicants.

### MICROSCOM

Programme advertises:
- up to 20 scholarships;
- full tuition;
- EUR1,400/month;
- 2027-29 application deadline 15 April 2027.

Its self-funded non-EU cost is far too high to treat as a financial fallback.

**Interpretation for all EMJM routes:** high-upside scholarship bets, never financial safeties.

## 3. Taiwan

The primary benchmark is the MOE Taiwan Scholarship:

- up to NTD40,000/semester for tuition/miscellaneous academic fees;
- NTD20,000/month for graduate students;
- Master's support up to 2 years.

Important: the NTD40,000 figure is a **cap**, not a universal tuition waiver.

### NTHU

Current tuition:
- Engineering approximately NTD36,300/semester;
- Life Sciences approximately NTD33,000/semester.

Both are below the MOE cap.

Therefore MOE gives a particularly clean tuition + living package at NTHU.

NTHU's own Master's scholarship instead offers:
- tuition/credit waiver;
- NTD5,000/month.

That university-only stipend is substantially weaker for living costs.

### NTU Taiwan

Current Engineering/EECS graduate tuition is around NTD60,720/semester.

With MOE:
- approximately NTD20,720/semester remains uncovered.

Smart Medicine and Health Informatics is much more expensive:
- roughly NTD100,000/semester;
- MOE leaves about NTD60,000/semester residual tuition.

Therefore Smart Medicine is financially weaker than NTU BEBI/BME under the same Taiwan Scholarship.

### NYCU

University scholarship can provide tuition reduction/waiver and stipend, but:

- graduate funding can be advisor/department matched;
- award size is less predictable;
- annual renewal matters;
- government and university awards generally cannot stack.

### TMU

Current Master's international scholarship examples:

- Type B: full tuition waiver + NTD12,000/month;
- Type C: full tuition waiver.

All admitted international applicants are considered.

### Yuan Ze

An unusually useful Taiwan-specific rule:

> Taiwan Scholarship recipients do not pay the portion of tuition above the MOE NTD40,000 cap.

This makes the Taiwan Scholarship much closer to a full tuition + NTD20k/month package at Yuan Ze.

### Taiwan Tech

Current full Master's scholarship:

- tuition/fees/credit-fees waived;
- NTD12,000/month;
- renewable subject to requirements.

MOE remains an alternative at NTD20,000/month.

Tsukuba's generic MEXT University Recommendation route is expected to be discontinued from 2027, while post-enrolment awards are less useful for a finance-constrained incoming student.

## 7. Italy

### Politecnico di Milano BME

Two strong routes can coexist:

**Polimi merit**
- full tuition waiver;
- some awards include up to roughly EUR10,000/year.

**Invest Your Talent in Italy 2026/27**
- Biomedical Engineering explicitly listed;
- EUR10,800 over 9 months;
- tuition exemption;
- renewable subject to progression;
- mandatory company internship in Italy.

Vietnam is an eligible IYT nationality.

IYT does not cover accommodation, travel or health insurance.

### University of Genoa Bioengineering

Also explicitly listed in the current IYT programme list.

Same current IYT financial structure:
- EUR10,800/9 months;
- tuition exemption.

### Bologna BME - reclassified C

ER.GO is means-tested rather than a merit full-funding competition.

For 2026/27, off-site grant values range by ISEE:

- EUR7,171.11 at ISEE <= EUR16,666.67;
- up to EUR8,246.78 at ISEE <= EUR12,500;
- lower amounts at higher eligible ISEE bands.

ER.GO can combine with tuition relief and regional-tax reimbursement.

**Interpretation:** financially resilient if household-economic eligibility is met, but not B-style competitive full funding.

### Milan/Polimi Bioinformatics for Computational Genomics - reclassified C

Important correction:

The current 2026/27 IYT course list includes Polimi Biomedical Engineering but **does not list BCG**.

Do not infer IYT eligibility merely because Politecnico di Milano is a partner university.

University of Milan provides international merit/exemption mechanisms, but a current non-EU full-living package for BCG is not verified.

Therefore BCG is financially resilient/conditional rather than strongly fundable.

## 9. France - BME Paris / Eiffel

Current Eiffel Master's support:

- EUR1,200/month;
- international and national transport;
- insurance;
- housing-search and other services.

Eiffel itself does not pay arbitrary programme costs.

However, French government scholarship holders are exempt from tuition for national Master's/accredited engineering degrees at eligible public institutions.

Application is **institution nominated**; students cannot submit directly to Campus France.

**Interpretation:** strong funded scenario, but nomination is a highly selective dependency and the exact BME Paris host/enrolment status must be verified.

Source:
- https://www.campusfrance.org/en/france-excellence-eiffel-scholarship-implementation

## 12. Technion BME MSc

Technion uses scholarship **portions**.

Current rules:

- each portion gives 25% tuition exemption;
- 4+ portions -> full tuition exemption;
- MSc support can run up to 24 months.

From April 2026:
- portion before research topic: NIS1,101/month;
- portion after research topic: NIS1,178/month.

Four portions therefore correspond to roughly NIS4.4k-4.7k/month plus full tuition exemption.

**Interpretation:** potentially strong, but the number of scholarship portions in the actual offer is decisive.

Source:
- https://graduate.technion.ac.il/

## B-band conclusion

B-band now has a consistent meaning:

> **worth applying when the scholarship/assistantship route is real and specific, but do not accept or financially plan around an unfunded offer.**

This is especially important for:

- NUS/NTU Singapore;
- Erasmus Mundus;
- TU Delft;
- SNU;
- Eiffel;
- UIUC;
- MBZUAI.

The current audit deliberately downgraded routes where a generic scholarship ecosystem was not enough evidence of strong programme-level fundability.
