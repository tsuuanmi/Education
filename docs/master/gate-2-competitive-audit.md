# Gate 2 - Competitive Funding Deep Audit

## Result

The 45 Gate-1 Likely routes with a credible but non-structural funding path split into:

| Competitive class | Count | Meaning |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| B1 - Full/near-full package if selected | 23 | The award itself is financially strong; the main risk is winning it. |
| B2 - Mixed / partial package | 19 | Tuition/stipend support is meaningful but may leave residual cost or require a university top-up / second funding route. |
| B3 - Assistantship / funding uncertain | 3 | Funding exists in principle but is not robust enough to model as a dependable full package. |

This classification evaluates **package quality**, not scholarship probability.

## B1 - Full-package competitive routes

### NUS / NTU Singapore research scholarships

**NUS Research Scholarship**

International Master's (Research):

- full tuition waiver;
- S$2,900/month;
- normally up to two years;
- no specific service bond;
- Graduate Assistantship duties.

**NTU Research Scholarship**

International Master's (Research):

- annual tuition covered;
- S$2,900/month from Jan 2026;
- up to two years;
- no bond;
- teaching/lab supervision duties for international students.

The package is excellent, but scholarship selection is distinct from simply being academically admissible.

### NTU VinGroup Graduate Scholarship

For Vietnamese Master's candidates, current award terms include:

- annual tuition and miscellaneous fees;
- S$1,500/month;
- S$1,500 settling allowance;
- S$500 airfare;
- up to two years for Master's candidates.

The stipend is lower than the NTU Research Scholarship. Programme-specific eligibility for MSc Biomedical Data Science must be reconfirmed for the relevant cycle before relying on this route.

### SNU GSFS

Current structure:

- full tuition for up to four semesters;
- at least KRW 500,000/month;
- living-support duration depends on college/school.

The Spring 2027 call selected 22 students university-wide, so the funding package is strong but quota-constrained.

### Erasmus Mundus

The EMJM scholarship architecture is financially strong when awarded: participation costs plus a living/travel support framework.

The earlier research already showed that award probability can be extremely low in popular programmes, so EMJM remains a **high-upside competitive bet**, not a safety.

### TU Delft departmental scholarships

The Microelectronics department states that some scholarships cover:

- full MSc tuition;
- contribution to living expenses;
- for two years.

A complete MSc application must be uploaded before 1 December for scholarship consideration.

The package is B1-quality; probability is the unresolved dimension.

### MEXT / Japan

MEXT routes generally have B1 package quality when won.

A particularly concrete 2027 example is NAIST's MEXT International Priority Graduate Program in Information Science:

- four Master's scholarships;
- JPY 144,000/month;
- tuition and entrance-fee exemption;
- airfare;
- Oct 2027-Sep 2029 term.

The small quota is why the programme remains B1 rather than A.

## B2 - Mixed or partial routes

### Taiwan

The 2026 MOE Taiwan Scholarship baseline is:

- tuition/miscellaneous subsidy up to NT$40,000 per semester;
- NT$20,000/month for graduate recipients;
- maximum two years for Master's.

Costs above the tuition cap, insurance and accommodation remain the student's responsibility.

Examples of university-level alternatives/top-ups:

- NTHU International Student Scholarship: tuition and credit-fee waiver + NT$5,000/month for Master's;
- NYCU can provide additional tuition grant beyond the MOE NT$40,000 cap in some cases.

Taiwan remains attractive, but the package should not be equated with Singapore/Hong Kong full research funding.

### France

**Eiffel 2027**

- €1,200/month from Jan 2026;
- transport, insurance and housing-search support;
- public national-diploma scholarship holders are exempt from registration tuition;
- other training/programme costs are not covered.

**Paris-Saclay International Master's Scholarship**

- €10,000/year;
- up to €900/year travel/visa support;
- automatic second-year renewal for successful M1 progression.

These are meaningful packages, but net cost depends on the specific institution/programme.

### Italy

Invest Your Talent in Italy 2026/27 provides:

- €10,800/year over 9 months;
- Vietnam is an eligible nationality;
- mandatory internship.

University merit scholarships, DSU/ER.GO need-based support and tuition waivers can materially improve the final package, but they must be modelled programme by programme.

### KU Leuven

Master Mind / ASEAN / faculty-level fee-waiver routes make the programme fundable, but the package is not a single standard research-studentship structure.

## B3 - Assistantship / funding uncertain

Current B3 candidates include:

- UIUC MS Bioengineering;
- University of Kansas MS Bioengineering;
- MBZUAI MSc Computational Biology.

These programmes have assistantship/fellowship routes, but current evidence does not justify assuming full funding at admission.

MBZUAI is especially important to keep current: earlier launch material used “fully funded” language, while current MSc admissions policy makes support conditional on a faculty-sponsored RA/TA or an approved scholarship.

## Financial interpretation

The competitive group should therefore be thought of as:

```text
B1:
great economics if won
-> evaluate probability / nomination / deadline

B2:
meaningful support
-> calculate residual cost and stacking rules

B3:
funding possible
-> do not rely on it before a concrete offer
```

This prevents a scholarship name from being treated as equivalent to a funding guarantee.
