# Education

Research and decision workspace for graduate education planning.

## Current focus

**Master's / research master's planning for 2027 and 2028 intake** with the long-term goal of becoming a **Research Engineer in Biological Systems**.

The target identity is intentionally broader than one modality:

```text
biological system
  -> measurement / sensing
  -> signals / data
  -> estimation / inference
  -> system / decision
  -> validated product or research artifact
```

BCI / neurotechnology is the current preferred application domain, while genomics remains an important second domain and source of research experience.

## Search philosophy

The search is **degree-title agnostic**.

A programme does not need to be called Biomedical Engineering. Electrical Engineering, Signals & Systems, Control, Robotics, Medical Engineering, Life Sciences Engineering, or a research degree can all be stronger options if they develop the right methodological core and allow relevant biological / health research.

We search broadly first and filter only after the opportunity universe is large enough.

## Decision process

1. Build the broadest realistic opportunity universe.
2. Gate 1: formal eligibility and prerequisites.
3. Gate 2: financial feasibility and net personal cost.
4. Gate 3: scholarship / bond / immigration / career constraints.
5. Gate 4: research and career fit.
6. Prestige is only a tie-breaker.

Finance is a hard constraint. Full tuition + living support is strongly preferred; low-tuition programs and need-based regional aid are also first-class paths.

## Evidence standard

- Prefer current official university, scholarship, and immigration sources.
- Record **TBA / unknown** instead of guessing.
- Distinguish hard prerequisites from recommended background.
- Do not assume work experience substitutes for formal academic credits unless the institution explicitly allows it.
- Do not rank until the opportunity universe is broad enough.
